Nature Communications · 2026

Machine learning-assisted self-powered ear tag for animal welfare

Xiaoyu Su, Peidi Fan, Ying Liu, Jianfeng Ping, Xunjia Li and Yuxiang Pan

Nature Communications 17, 7094 (2026) · Published 02 June 2026 · DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-73651-7

The study presents an energy-autonomous livestock ear tag that combines motion-based and solar energy harvesting with microneedle sensors. The system tracks pH, potassium and calcium in interstitial fluid, then uses machine learning to interpret the signals as welfare-related metabolic states.

The authors report average classification accuracy above 95% across five states, evaluated on 5,399 sampling windows from three animals using leave-one-animal-out validation. These results describe the study’s experimental cohort and are independent of the RIOSE product.
